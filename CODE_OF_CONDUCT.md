# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes; and
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention,
trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, publishing another person's private
information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's private
vulnerability-reporting route described in `SECURITY.md`. Do not put sensitive
personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will protect the privacy
and safety of reporters as far as practical and will respond proportionately
to the context and impact.
